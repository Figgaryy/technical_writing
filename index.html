Recommendation Report: Selecting a Battery Pack for the First-Year Electronics Laboratory

Introduction
The Electrical Engineering Department needs to purchase rechargeable battery packs for its new first-year electronics laboratory. Students with limited experience will use the packs several times a week and move them between workstations. As requested by the Laboratory Coordinator, this report compares three 12 V products, VoltPro 12, PowerCell 12, and EnerGo 12, and recommends one for purchase. The evidence comes from the coordinator's memo, two supplier emails, and one specification sheet.
Introductiondecision → users and problem → solicited + options → sources
Recommendation
We recommend that the department purchase the EnerGo 12. It is the only product that lists overheating protection in addition to overcharge and short-circuit protection. Although it has the highest unit price, it has the lowest cost per charge cycle and the longest expected life. It also charges fastest and shows clearly when charging is complete.
Recommendationตัวที่เลือก แล้วสรุปเหตุผลเรียงตาม criteria 1→4 พร้อมยอมรับจุดด้อย
Scope and Criteria
The products were evaluated against four criteria in the order ranked by the coordinator. Safety is the first criterion because inexperienced students will charge and handle the units often. Cost is ranked second because the equipment budget is limited; it includes both the purchase price and the expected service life. Useful life is third because longer-lasting batteries reduce replacements and interruptions to classes. Ease of use is fourth and covers weight, charging time, and the charging-status indicator. All product figures come from the suppliers and have not been independently tested.
Scoperanked + เหตุผลทุกข้อ + นิยามว่า criterion ครอบคลุมอะไร + limitation
Discussion
Safety
The department needs a battery with suitable protection for routine charging and use. All three products have overcharge and short-circuit protection, and EnerGo 12 is the only one that also lists overheating protection. PowerCell's sheet does not list this feature, but it states that the omission does not prove the product is unsafe. Because no supplier provided independent safety tests, the specifications alone cannot show that EnerGo 12 is safer overall. However, the additional listed protection is an advantage for new users, so EnerGo 12 is preferred for safety.
Discussion · C1requirement → เทียบ → limitation ของหลักฐาน → ผู้ชนะ
Cost
The department needs a product that fits a limited budget over its whole service life. VoltPro 12 has the lowest unit price at $48, compared with $55 for PowerCell 12 and $62 for EnerGo 12. However, when the price is divided by the expected number of cycles, the cost per cycle is about $0.096 for VoltPro 12, $0.069 for PowerCell 12, and $0.062 for EnerGo 12. The cheapest unit is therefore the most expensive to use over time. Prices may change with order size, and EnerGo's quoted price applies only to orders of fewer than 20 units. EnerGo 12 offers the best long-term value, while VoltPro 12 has the lowest initial cost.
Discussion · C2ตัวเลขที่คำนวณเอง = การตีความข้อมูล
Useful Life
The batteries must last through repeated use during the teaching term. EnerGo 12 is rated for about 1,000 cycles, PowerCell 12 for about 800, and VoltPro 12 for about 500. EnerGo 12 and PowerCell 12 also include a two-year warranty, while VoltPro 12 includes only one year. These cycle figures are estimates, not guarantees. Even so, EnerGo 12 is expected to last the longest.
Discussion · C3hedge: estimates, not guarantees
Ease of Use
Students and technicians must be able to carry the units, see when charging is complete, and prepare them for each session. VoltPro 12 is the lightest at 1.4 kg, but it takes about five hours to charge and has no status display beyond the charger light. PowerCell 12 is the heaviest at 1.7 kg and charges in about four hours with a simple indicator. EnerGo 12 weighs 1.5 kg, charges in about three hours, and has a light that changes colour when charging is complete. EnerGo 12 is therefore the easiest to prepare between classes, although VoltPro 12 is lighter.
Discussion · C4ครบทั้ง 3 ตัวแปรที่ memo ระบุ
Conclusion
EnerGo 12 is the best choice for the first-year laboratory because it leads on listed safety features, cost per cycle, useful life, and charging time. Its main trade-off is the highest unit price, which raises the initial cost of a class set. Because all figures come from suppliers, the department should confirm the final price for its order size before purchasing.

Recommendation Report: Replacing the Classroom Projectors in Building 3

Introduction
Although no one has requested this report, the IT Support Unit has noticed that the projectors in Building 3 are now disrupting teaching. The twelve classrooms still use lamp projectors installed in 2019. Last semester, these projectors failed 19 times during class, and 31 sessions were delayed or moved to another room. The unit also spent 96,000 THB on replacement lamps. If nothing changes, failures are likely to increase as the units age. This report compares two possible replacements, the LumaView LX5 lamp projector and the BrightBeam L300 laser projector, against three criteria: reliability, five-year cost, and image brightness.
Introductionบอกว่าไม่มีใครขอ → ปัญหาพร้อมตัวเลข → ผลถ้าไม่แก้ → ทางเลือก + criteria · ยังไม่บอกว่าแนะนำตัวไหน
Scope
The criteria are ranked by their effect on teaching, which is the Faculty's first priority. Reliability is ranked first because a failed projector stops a class immediately. It covers the expected life of the light source and the parts that need regular replacement. Five-year cost is second because the Faculty's equipment budget is fixed; it includes the purchase price, replacement lamps, and electricity, based on about 1,600 hours of use per year. Image brightness is third because students in the back rows must be able to read slides in a lit room. Product figures come from supplier specification sheets, and usage figures come from the unit's own repair log.
Scoperanked + เหตุผลผูกกับเป้าหมายของ Faculty + นิยาม criterion + แหล่งข้อมูล
Discussion
Reliability
Each classroom needs a projector that runs through a full semester without failing. According to the repair log, 17 of last semester's 19 failures were lamp failures. The LX5 lamp is rated for 3,500 hours, so each unit would need a new lamp about every two years. The L300 uses a laser light source rated for 20,000 hours, which is longer than the five-year period in this report, and it has no lamp or air filter to replace. Supplier ratings are not guarantees, but the L300 removes the part responsible for most current failures. The L300 is therefore the stronger option for reliability.
Discussion · C1requirement → หลักฐานจาก repair log → เทียบ → hedge → ผู้ชนะ
Five-Year Cost
The Faculty needs a solution that it can afford over the life of the equipment, not only at purchase. The LX5 costs 28,000 THB per unit, compared with 42,000 THB for the L300. Over five years, however, the LX5 needs two replacement lamps at 4,000 THB each and uses 280 W, while the L300 needs no lamps and uses 200 W. At 4.50 THB per kWh, the five-year cost per classroom is about 46,100 THB for the LX5 and 49,200 THB for the L300. One concern may be the higher purchase price, which raises the initial cost for twelve rooms from 336,000 THB to 504,000 THB. However, the difference over five years is only about 3,100 THB per room, and this estimate does not include technician time or lost class time. The LX5 is cheaper, but the gap is small.
Discussion · C2คำนวณให้เห็น → ตอบข้อโต้แย้ง "แพงกว่า" ก่อนผู้อ่านยก → ยอมรับว่าแพ้ใน criterion นี้
Image Brightness
Students must be able to read text from the back of a classroom with the lights on. Both models are rated at 4,000 lumens when new. However, a lamp loses brightness as it ages, and the LX5 sheet states that output falls to about 50 percent by the end of the lamp's life. The L300 is rated to keep about 80 percent of its brightness after 20,000 hours. The L300 will therefore stay brighter for longer.
Discussion · C3ค่าเท่ากันตอนใหม่ จึงต้องดูต่อว่าเปลี่ยนไปอย่างไรตามอายุ
Recommendation
Based on this comparison, we recommend that the Faculty replace the Building 3 projectors with the BrightBeam L300. It is the more reliable option because it removes the lamp, which caused most failures last semester, and it keeps its brightness longer. Its main drawback is the higher purchase price, but over five years it costs only about 3,100 THB more per room than the LX5. As a first step, we suggest installing the L300 in the two most-used classrooms this semester for 84,000 THB and comparing their repair records with the other rooms before replacing the rest.

ตัวอย่างเต็มฉบับ · Week 13 Chiller Replacement (≈600 คำ)
TO:SIIT Facilities ManagerFROM:[Your name], Engineering Laboratory StaffDATE:25 November 2026SUBJECT:Proposal to Replace the Engineering Laboratory Chiller
Problem
The air-conditioning system in one SIIT engineering laboratory uses a large amount of electricity and cannot keep the room within the required temperature range. The 12-year-old chiller runs at a fixed speed even when little cooling is needed. I propose replacing it with a variable-speed, high-efficiency air-cooled chiller, which is estimated to cut electricity costs by about 135,000 THB per year.
Problemปัญหา (present simple) → สาเหตุ → ทางแก้ 1 ประโยค + ตัวเลขดึงความสนใจ
Background
The laboratory uses a fixed-speed, air-cooled chiller connected to an air-handling unit (AHU). The chiller produces cold water, and the AHU uses this water to cool and circulate air. Because the compressor mainly runs at one speed, the system uses more electricity than necessary when demand is low. Table 1 summarizes the results of the internal energy audit.
Backgroundอธิบายกลไกของปัญหา แล้วนำเข้าตาราง
Table 1. Audit results for the current cooling system
System age12 years
Annual electricity use120,000 kWh
Electricity tariff4.50 THB/kWh
Annual electricity cost540,000 THB
Annual maintenance cost45,000 THB
Observed room temperature20–27 °C
Required room temperature22–24 °C
Unplanned downtime30 hours/year
Tableโจทย์สั่งให้มีตารางหรือกราฟ · ใส่ชื่อ Table 1 และหน่วยทุกแถว
As Table 1 shows, the room temperature sometimes moves outside the required 22–24 °C range, and maintenance problems cause 30 hours of unplanned downtime each year. If the problem continues, electricity and maintenance costs will remain high, unstable temperatures may damage equipment or affect experiments, and further breakdowns may interrupt teaching and research.
Backgroundตีความตาราง → ผลถ้าไม่แก้ (โจทย์สั่ง)
Solutions
The proposed system is a variable-speed, high-efficiency air-cooled chiller from Sahakijanan International Co., Ltd. (VICH), connected to an AHU. Unlike the current chiller, it can change its compressor speed to match cooling demand, so it would reduce its output when less cooling is needed. Sensors and automatic controls would adjust the cooling output to keep the laboratory at 22–24 °C. Before installation, the supplier would confirm the laboratory's cooling requirements, including equipment heat, humidity, and ventilation, so that the system is neither too small nor too large. The estimated cost of the equipment, installation, and testing is 450,000 THB.
Solutionwhat → how it works → เงื่อนไขก่อนติดตั้ง → cost
Benefits
Energy: The supplier estimates that the new system would reduce annual electricity use by 25%, from 120,000 kWh to 90,000 kWh, a saving of 30,000 kWh. Financial: At 4.50 THB per kWh, this would save about 135,000 THB per year (30,000 × 4.50). The simple payback period would therefore be about 3.3 years (450,000 ÷ 135,000). Maintenance costs might also fall from 45,000 to 30,000 THB per year, which would shorten the payback period to about three years, but this saving is not guaranteed. Operational: Automatic controls would help keep the room within the required temperature range, which would protect equipment and experiments. Fewer breakdowns could also reduce interruptions, although the expected downtime has not yet been confirmed. These figures are estimates, not guaranteed results. SIIT should request a detailed quotation and measure electricity use and room temperature after installation to check performance.
Benefitsenergy / financial / operational ตามที่โจทย์แบ่ง + การคำนวณ + hedge
Conclusion
The current chiller wastes electricity and cannot keep the laboratory within the required temperature range. Replacing it with a variable-speed chiller would cost about 450,000 THB and could save about 135,000 THB in electricity each year, so it would pay for itself in about 3.3 years while improving temperature control. I hope you will approve a request for a detailed quotation from VICH so that the replacement can proceed once the laboratory's cooling requirements are confirmed.
Conclusionproblem → solution + cost → benefit → action
